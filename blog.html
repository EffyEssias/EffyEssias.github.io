A LITTLE ABOUT ME✦

The worlds that
made me dream.

I've been making art since I was a child. Even then, I imagined a better future: one where people and nature could live in peace, and anyone could be anything.

The world around me shaped those dreams, and games gave them somewhere to live. Club Penguin was a big inspiration. It helped me fall in love with the idea of making playful places where people could explore, connect, and express themselves.

As I grew, that love grew with me. I started making art and exploring game development because I wanted to build worlds of my own—worlds that feel welcoming, imaginative, and full of possibility.

I hope I can inspire someone the way I was inspired, and share art that helps people express themselves or find a little comfort. My little brother and my animals are a big part of why I keep working hard.
