Hey, that's me!

Hi, I'm Effy.

I've been making art since I was a child. The world around me shaped how I imagine things: a better future where people and nature live in peace, and anyone can be anything.

Inspired by Club Penguin, I fell in love with game development. That love grew with me, and my dream of making welcoming worlds grew alongside it.

I want to inspire others the way I was inspired, and share art that helps people express themselves and find comfort. I work hard for my little brother and my animals—they're at the heart of what keeps me going.
